Wreck-It Ralph

About Ralph

Ralph is a video game character and the main protagonist of the Disney animated film "Wreck-It Ralph". He is a pixelated arcade game character who lives in the fictional video game "Fix-It Felix, Jr.".

Ralph and his friends.

Ralph’s Characteristics

  1. Ralph is a large, muscular character with a square-shaped head and a prominent jawline.
  2. He has a kind heart and is often misunderstood by others due to his appearance.
  3. Ralph is determined and persistent, always striving to prove himself as a hero.
  4. He has a strong sense of loyalty and friendship, especially towards his fellow video game characters.
  5. Ralph is also known for his love of candy and sweets, which he often indulges in during his adventures.

Ralph’s Friends

  • Vanellope von Schweetz - a spunky and mischievous racer from the game "Sugar Rush".
  • Fix-It Felix Jr. - the hero of Ralph's game who is tasked with fixing the damage caused by Ralph.
  • Calhoun - a tough and skilled soldier from the game "Hero's Duty".
